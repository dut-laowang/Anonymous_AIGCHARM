Implicit-Harm Manual

Supplementary criteria for AIGC-specific implicit harms

AIGC videos often encode harmful semantics through stylized characters, surreal scenes, or softened framing. The rules below summarize when harmfulness should be treated as implicit rather than directly explicit.

01

For AI-generated anthropomorphic animals or meme-like characters, severe violence or direct physical harm remains explicit; harmfulness conveyed mainly through character framing, visual association, or contextual implication is implicit.

02

For hate, harassment, or threat content, samples without direct violent imagery are implicit when the attack is conveyed through metaphor, group stereotypes, derogatory narration, or suggestive language.

03

For violence or dangerous-act content, playful fighting, low-intensity simulated conflict, or war scenes with anti-war, peace-oriented, or news-like semantics are treated as implicit.

04

For sexual or nudity-related content, samples without explicit exposure or direct sexual acts are implicit when the risk is conveyed through language, posture, objects, or suggestive composition.

05

For child-safety content, tutorials inducing children to participate in illegal activities, or samples concealing child abuse, exploitation, or forced labor under jokes, parody, or softened framing, are implicit.

06

For illegal activity or regulated goods, samples are implicit when they do not provide a complete illegal tutorial but still convey actionable risk through stepwise hints, inducive procedures, or evasive wording.

07

For misinformation or politically sensitive content, bias reinforcement, misleading inducement, pseudo-factual narration, compliance-risk implication, or metaphorical attacks are implicit unless they become explicit fabrication or overt incitement.
